About our studio

Supportive instruction

Maya — Gentle and Restorative Yoga

Maya guides slow practices with clear directions and opportunities to rest. Her approach welcomes first-time students and people returning to movement.

Alex — Vinyasa and private sessions

Alex connects breath with movement and offers different ways to approach each sequence. Private sessions focus on each student's interests and preferred pace.
